# KATY IN CONTROL: THE GENESIS — PERFORMANCE SCRIPT (KATY REAL ONLY)
## For solo recording — Twin will be composited in later (HeyGen + CapCut)

**How to use this document:** Only your lines are here. Each block shows *(in italics, bracketed)* a short paraphrase of what Twin says right before or after — that's your cue, not something to perform. Twin will sit **screen-right** in the final composite, so "look right" = look toward where she'll be. "To camera" = look straight into the lens, talking to the audience, not to Twin.

Pause markers `(beat)` = a real pause, not filler. Don't rush them — they land better slower than you'd think on the day.

### ACT 2 — THE SNAP (medical odyssey)

> *[Cue: Twin is narrating the Halloween/badminton scene, gets to "somewhere inside her right ankle —"]*

**Interject sharply, almost cutting Twin off. This is the big comedic beat of the episode — commit fully, a little theatrical.**

> *[Cue: Twin narrates the husband examining the shoe]*

**Switch into "husband voice" — exaggerated, mock-serious, puff your chest a little. Clearly a caricature, not mimicry.**

> "Your sole is broken. That's the snap you heard."

`(back to your normal voice/posture immediately after — no lingering in the bit)`

> *[Cue: Twin runs through the montage of doctors — sprain diagnosis, "why not" physio, ultrasound on the wrong spot, then sets up the ski trip]*

**"Surgeon voice" — brief, breezy, very French shrug energy.**

**Break the fourth wall of the narration itself — turn toward Twin, a little smug.**

> *[Cue: Twin, mock offended — "Without you, I don't have a voice to tell it with. Technically."]*

> Exactly. Remember that.

### ACT 3 — THE PIVOT

> *[Cue: this is your act — you carry the whole thing solo, no Twin interruptions until the very end]*

**To camera. Matter-of-fact at first, almost clinical — like reading off a medical chart.**

> Surgery. March 10th. Forty-five days, fully immobilized. Foot up. Zero weight-bearing. Daily injections.

`(beat)`

**Shift tone — this is the emotional core of the episode. Still light, but honest. Don't perform it as a joke.**

> Here's the thing about me: I run non-stop. Thousands of ideas — most of them good, I think. Stillness makes me a little unhinged if I can't at least build something.

`(beat)`

> And now I couldn't move. At all.

`(beat — let the stakes sit for a second)`

**Wry smile creeping back in.**

> So — two days in — I did what any reasonable engineer does in a personal crisis.

> *[Cue: Twin, deadpan — "She blew her CPF."]*

**To camera, slightly explaining/aside tone — you're briefing an international audience on a French thing.**

> In France, everyone gets a CPF — a Compte Personnel de Formation, a state-funded training account. Mine had been sitting there, untouched, for years.

`(beat)`

**Building energy — this is the punchline pairing with "snap."**

> My Achilles went snap. Forty-eight hours into total stillness — I snapped too. Blew the whole thing on an AI training program.

> *[Cue: Twin — "Two certifications later... she wasn't just filling time."]*

**Slower again. Genuinely sincere — tap your temple on the line, don't skip that gesture, it's doing real work.**

> I found a prosthesis. Not for the ankle. For this.

`(tap temple)`

> Thousands of ideas that lived perfectly well out loud — in a meeting, in a hallway, in my own head at 2am — and died the second I tried to put them on paper. The AI didn't replace the thinking. It finally gave it somewhere to land.

## RECORDING NOTES

- **Release cadence:** all 3 parts drop every 2-3 days, not weekly, not all at once — keeps the narrative thread warm without dead air between episodes.
- **Look — deliberately different between the two of you:** Twin = polished, professional (blazer, styled hair) to sell "the perfected version." Real Katy = casual, authentic (the one with the accent, the tendon, the texts to Alain). The contrast is doing narrative work, not just aesthetic — lean into it.
- **Eye-line consistency:** every "look right" cue assumes Twin sits screen-right in the final composite. If you flip that in CapCut, mentally mirror all the directions above.
- **Part III is almost entirely Twin.** Don't record filler for the tour/roadmap/thank-you sections — that's 100% HeyGen. You only appear at the very end.
- **The "SNAP" line in Part I Act 2** is the single most important comedic beat in the trilogy — worth a few takes to get the timing right against where Twin's narration will sit.
- **French closing line in Part III** is the tonal outlier of the whole trilogy — resist the urge to keep it light/funny. It's the one unguarded moment.
