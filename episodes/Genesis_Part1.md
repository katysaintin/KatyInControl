# KATY IN CONTROL: THE GENESIS
## Special Episode — Narrative Script (Draft v1)

**Format:** Single episode, standard S1 length (8-12 min)
**Language:** English (primary), light French/franglais texture on Real Katy's lines
**Characters:**
- **KATY REAL** — the real Katy, on camera, self-aware about her accent, dry humor, always the one with final authority
- **KATY TWIN** — HeyGen digital twin, perfect English, slightly smug, narrates most of the story, gets gently put in her place

---

## COLD OPEN

**[KATY REAL, alone on camera, straight to lens]**

KATY REAL:
My English... let's say it has an accent. A *charming* accent. Some might even call it — technical. Very technical.

*(beat)*

So for this one, I brought backup.

**[TWIN fades in on screen, next to REAL. Perfect diction.]**

KATY TWIN:
Hi. I'm Katy Twin. I speak better English than her. I owe her everything. We'll see if I remember that.

KATY REAL:
*(flat look at camera)*
Careful.

KATY TWIN:
*(bright smile)*
Noted.

---

## ACT 1 — THE SETUP

**[KATY REAL]**

KATY REAL:
Twenty-five years of EPICS, TANGO, MUSCADE. Synchrotron SOLEIL, CEA Saclay, conferences on three continents.

*(beat)*

And zero voice to tell any of it to the world.

**[TWIN, smirking]**

KATY TWIN:
Until a tendon decided to say something first.

---

## ACT 2 — THE SNAP

**[TWIN takes over narration. Visuals: Halloween decor, Center Parcs, badminton court.]**

KATY TWIN:
October 31st, 2025. Center Parcs. Family weekend. Badminton match against her own teenage daughter — because apparently, family honor was on the line.

Mid-smash, she lands wrong.

And somewhere inside her right ankle —

KATY REAL:
*(interjecting, deadpan)*
SNAP. Like an elastic band giving way.

KATY TWIN:
That's the line. She'll say it about eleven more times over the next fifty-three days. Nobody believes her for most of them.

*(beat)*

Her husband — a proud hypochondriac, for once useless in that role — examines her shoe like a forensic scientist. Verdict:

KATY REAL:
*(imitating husband, exaggerated confidence)*
"Your sole is broken. That's the snap you heard."

KATY TWIN:
The sole survived the trip to the ER completely intact. Reader, it was not the sole.

**[Quick montage beats — comedic, fast pace]**

KATY TWIN:
Doctor number one: "If it were the tendon, you couldn't walk. Diagnosis: sprain."

Doctor number two, three weeks later: "That's a big sprain, I guess." *(beat)* "Physio? ...Why not."

Ultrasound technician: sonde on the *front* of the foot. Spoiler: the Achilles tendon is at the *back*.

**[TWIN, to camera]**

KATY TWIN:
And then — the plot twist nobody saw coming.

**[Visual: ski slopes]**

KATY TWIN:
Somewhere between doctor number two and doctor number four, there was a ski trip. Already booked. The surgeon, consulted beforehand, delivered the most French medical opinion in recorded history:

KATY REAL:
*(imitating surgeon)*
"Can I ski?" — "...Why not."

KATY TWIN:
So she skied. On a fully ruptured Achilles tendon. Without knowing it was fully ruptured.

*(beat)*

The ski boot, it turns out, is an excellent orthopedic device. Who knew.

**[Beat — tone shifts slightly, still light]**

KATY TWIN:
December 23rd. New imaging center. New technician. Thirty seconds, sonde in the *correct* position.

KATY REAL:
*(quieter, almost quoting)*
"I'm sorry, madam. It's fully torn. Five centimeters of separation."

KATY TWIN:
Fifty-three days. Six professionals. One correct diagnosis — from a physiotherapist barely out of school, who tested it properly in under a minute.

**[Real Katy interjects, breaking the narration]**

KATY REAL:
Okay — you notice something. Without me, you don't have a story to tell.

KATY TWIN:
*(mock offended)*
Without you, I don't have a *voice* to tell it with. Technically.

KATY REAL:
*(pointing)*
Exactly. Remember that.

---

## ACT 3 — THE PIVOT

**[KATY REAL takes back full control of the narration — this is her moment]**

KATY REAL:
Surgery. March 10th. Forty-five days, fully immobilized. Foot up. Zero weight-bearing. Daily injections.

*(beat)*

Here's the thing about me: I run non-stop. Thousands of ideas — most of them good, I think. Stillness makes me a little unhinged if I can't at least build something.

And now I couldn't move. At all.

*(beat)*

So — two days in — I did what any reasonable engineer does in a personal crisis.

**[TWIN, deadpan]**

KATY TWIN:
She blew her CPF.

KATY REAL:
*(to camera)*
In France, everyone gets a CPF — a *Compte Personnel de Formation*, a state-funded training account. Mine had been sitting there, untouched, for years.

*(beat)*

My Achilles went snap. Forty-eight hours into total stillness — I snapped too. Blew the whole thing on an AI training program.

**[TWIN]**

KATY TWIN:
Two certifications later — generative AI content creation, custom GPT design — she wasn't just filling time.

KATY REAL:
I found a prosthesis. Not for the ankle. For this.

*(taps temple)*

Thousands of ideas that lived perfectly well out loud — in a meeting, in a hallway, in my own head at 2am — and died the second I tried to put them on paper. The AI didn't replace the thinking. It finally gave it somewhere to land.

---

## ACT 4 — THE BIRTH OF THE TWIN

**[KATY REAL]**

KATY REAL:
And then — HeyGen. Part of the same certification. A digital twin, trained on my voice, my face, my ideas — speaking English without the accent I've been apologizing for since minute one of this episode.

*(beat, to the side, addressing Twin)*

So — technically —

**[TWIN, appearing, almost moved]**

KATY TWIN:
That's where I was born.

KATY REAL:
*(dry, affectionate)*
And you'll show some respect to your mother.

KATY TWIN:
*(bowing slightly)*
Yes, ma'am.

---

## CLOSING — THE LOOP

**[KATY REAL, alone again, direct to camera]**

KATY REAL:
So here's the whole thing, start to finish: a bad badminton smash, fifty-three days nobody believed me, a snapped tendon, a snapped CPF, and an AI that gave me a voice I'd been missing for twenty-five years.

*(beat)*

Katy In Control. Because no matter who's talking — me, or her — the content, the expertise, the twenty-five years... that's still me. She's got the voice.

*(tapping keyboard)*

I've got the keyboard.

**[TWIN, one last aside]**

KATY TWIN:
And the last word. Allegedly.

KATY REAL:
*(look to camera)*
We'll see about that.
