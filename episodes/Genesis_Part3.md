## ACT 1 — THE GUIDED TOUR

KATY TWIN:
Five pages. No clutter. Here's what's behind each one.

**HOME** — the front door. One mission statement, one honest sentence: making complex control systems understandable, human, and attractive. If you remember nothing else from this site, remember that line.

**EPISODES** — every lesson, in order. Beginner-friendly first, more technical as the seasons go on. Each one comes with a companion guide, because not everyone learns the same way, and nobody should have to pretend otherwise.

**CONTRIBUTORS** — the people who actually built EPICS, TANGO, and MUSCADE. Founders. Mentors. A few in memoriam. This page exists because history with no names in it isn't history — it's marketing.

**STARGÅTE** — still under construction, still teased. A documentation portal to make onboarding into this field less of a maze. More on that when it's ready.

**ABOUT** — the philosophy, and now, thanks to the last twenty minutes of your life — a bit more depth than the text alone ever gave you.

*(beat)*

One more thing, structurally, for the curious: the whole site runs on JSON as a single source of truth. Change a color once, it updates everywhere. No copy-pasted CSS haunting twelve different pages. Boring to say out loud. Very satisfying to maintain.

## ACT 2 — WHAT'S NEXT

KATY TWIN:
Here's where the seasons are headed.

**Season One** — where we are now. Accessible. Vulgarized. Built for Padawans walking into SCADA for the first time, from either side of the EPICS/TANGO divide.

**Season Two** — same ecosystem, more depth. Every piece we introduced gently in Season One gets a proper technical pass.

**Season Three** — a second subject enters the room: AI. Not as a buzzword — as an actual second area of expertise. Security, operational processes, HMI best practices, and how AI actually integrates into a SCADA environment, not just around it. There'll also be interviews — real experts, brought in — but always on one condition: stay accessible. This was never meant to be a standard tech talk. It's memory. It's transmission. It's for whoever's walking in next.
