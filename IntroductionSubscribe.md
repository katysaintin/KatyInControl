# INTRO - 1 minute

I have to confess something. I started with TANGO. Then I moved to EPICS. In Star Wars terms — I crossed to the Dark Side. And came back. Which makes me either a Jedi Master… or just someone who really likes control systems.
(pause)
Today I won’t tell you which side to choose. I’ll give you what I wish I’d had 20 years ago: a map of both galaxies.
(pause)
My name is Katy Saintin. I work at CEA IRFU, and before that I spent 13 years at Synchrotron SOLEIL. I’ve run TANGO in production, then EPICS in production, and today both in parallel — on real machines, real hardware, real deadlines.
I’m not a theorist. I’m not here to sell you anything. 

I’m here because after 20 years, I’ve noticed something: both communities are brilliant, both frameworks are powerful — and both are almost completely ignoring each other.
That gap has a cost. And today, together, we’re going to start closing it.
