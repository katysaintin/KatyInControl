About Katy

My name is Katy Saintin. I work in France at CEA IRFU, and before that I spent 13 years at Synchrotron SOLEIL.

I've been nominated Expert Senior SCADA since September 2025.

Over the years, I've worked on accelerator control systems, SCADA, HMI, EPICS, TANGO and Java software development, delivering technical solutions for demanding scientific environments.

Beyond the technical contributions, being a woman in this domain carries an important responsibility: increasing visibility and encouraging others to step into these fields.
